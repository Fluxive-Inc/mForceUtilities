Fluxive Deep Tech Logo Analysis
================================

FILE: logo_deep_tech.svg
DIMENSIONS: 512x512px

VISUAL COMPOSITION:
-------------------
The logo consists of three main geometric elements arranged symmetrically:

1.  Enclosing Brackets (Parentheses):
    -   Two large, rounded brackets flank the central element.
    -   Style: Thick, rounded strokes (Stroke-Width: 36).
    -   Vibe: Evokes code, containment, or a lens focusing on the center.

2.  Central Split Chevron/Arrowhead:
    -   An upward-pointing arrowhead shape located centrally between the brackets.
    -   Split vertically into two distinct halves:
        -   LEFT Half: Wireframe style. Outline only (No fill). Represents the "blueprint," "structure," or "unseen" data.
        -   RIGHT Half: Solid style. Filled with the gradient. Represents the "manifestation," "product," or "reality."

COLOR PALETTE & STYLING:
------------------------
1.  "Tech Gradient" (Vertical Linear Gradient):
    -   The defining color scheme for all elements.
    -   Top (0%): #FFFFFF (Pure White) - Represents light, energy, or the cloud/ether.
    -   Bottom (100%): #3B82F6 (Royal Blue) - Represents depth, stability, and technology.
    -   Effect: Provides a sleek, metallic, or illuminated look that fades from bright white at the top to deep blue at the bottom.

2.  Effects:
    -   Soft Drop Shadow ("Glow"): A subtle black shadow (Opacity: 0.5, Blur: 2px) gives depth and lifts the elements off the background.

DESIGN LANGUAGE:
----------------
-   "Deep Tech": The combination of wireframe and solid shapes suggests the bridge between theoretical infrastructure (wireframe) and tangible application (solid).
-   Gradient Usage: The vertical fade gives a "monolith" or "holographic" feel typical of modern SaaS and AI branding.
